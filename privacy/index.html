Delta Owl Eyes operates deltaowleyes.ca. We respect visitors' privacy and aim to collect only information needed to operate and improve the website.

Website hosting

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and device or browser information to deliver the site and maintain security. This processing is separate from our optional analytics choices. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Optional Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-C9BHRFJ22Z) to understand aggregate website use, including page visits, approximate location, device type and traffic sources. We load Google Analytics only after you choose “Accept analytics.” If you reject, our website does not load our Google Analytics tracking script. Google may process analytics data under its own terms and policies, potentially outside Canada. Learn more in Google's privacy policy.

Your analytics choice

Analytics is optional. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we can remember it. You can change your choice using the “Analytics preferences” link in the footer of each page. If you accepted previously and wish to withdraw, select “Reject analytics.” This prevents future analytics collection by our site, but it does not erase information already received by Google or automatically delete previously stored Google cookies. You can clear site cookies in your browser and contact us about a data request.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, request business listing verification, or ask about services, we may receive the contact information and details you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, manage listings, or deliver requested services. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless necessary.

External links and embedded services

Our pages may link to third-party websites or load external media and assets. Those providers may process technical information when their resources load, independently of your Google Analytics preference. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Retention and requests

We retain correspondence and business records as needed for their purpose and applicable obligations. Google Analytics retention settings are controlled in our Google Analytics account. You may ask us about information you have provided, request corrections, or request deletion where applicable.
