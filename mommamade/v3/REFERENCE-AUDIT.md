## QA thresholds
- Run responsive Chromium checks at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels; inspect 10 full-page desktop/mobile screenshots plus gallery.
- Verify zero horizontal overflow, legible primary headings, hero media dimension, distinctness of composition, all four product links, search/filter, keyboard-accessible navigation, simulated contact form and disabled checkout.
- Compare actual reference/local screenshots at matching viewports. If any reference blocks access or changes, report **unverified** and do not claim an exact match.
- Price/care descriptions and staff statements are illustrative and must not be converted into confirmed claims.
- Keep V2 as current unless approved, screenshots inspected, and deployment verified. Candidate registry status must remain `candidate` until then.
