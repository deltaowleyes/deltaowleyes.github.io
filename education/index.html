Help protect and improve this directory

Is there a public Indigenous learning resource we should include? Is a listing inaccurate, withdrawn or shared without appropriate cultural permission? Send a request directly to our editorial inbox; nothing is submitted or stored by this website.

Suggest a public resource Request a correction Request review or removal

Community knowledge and consent come first. Do not email private ceremonial teachings, recordings, photographs of children, documents with personal details, or material you do not have permission to share. For sensitive concerns, send only the public listing link and request a private conversation.

These links open your own email app; they do not publish submissions. Staff review publicly supported facts before changing the directory. An emailed nomination is not automatic approval.

Care, consent and accuracy: before opening a resource

Survivor testimony may include accounts of child removal, violence, loss and trauma. Choose when and how you engage. Some language, ceremonial or community knowledge is not intended for unrestricted circulation: please follow the source's instructions. Each listing identifies the original provider and source-review date, not guaranteed current admission, availability or cost.

To recommend a resource, correct a link or request removal, contact deltaowleyes@gmail.com. Please do not submit private cultural knowledge or personal details without permission.
