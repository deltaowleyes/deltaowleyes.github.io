Why Delta Owl Eyes?

Rooted in a connection to Bkejwanong (Walpole Island), Delta Owl Eyes aims to bring attention to important stories, amplify learning, and encourage informed action. We approach difficult subjects with curiosity, respect, and a commitment to distinguishing evidence from opinion.

Our name reflects the delta landscape and the importance of looking carefully at issues from more than one perspective. We are an independent media initiative, not an official representative of Walpole Island First Nation.
