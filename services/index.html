Our latest announcement

We're developing news and investigations, a dedicated educational and accountability resource for all 94 Truth and Reconciliation Commission Calls to Action, and an Indigenous Business Directory that helps people discover and support Indigenous-owned businesses across Canada.

Coming soon: The Owl's Lens

Our upcoming 94 Calls to Action video series will examine what each call demands, the history behind it, documented progress, outstanding challenges, and who is responsible for action. We aim to ground every episode in verified information and community perspectives.
