# Delta Owl Eyes — 94 Calls to Action script production standard

Updated 2026-10-29. Applies to all 94 episode rewrites. This is an editorial specification, not a claim that the scripts have already been revised.

- **Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds to 4 minutes, verified by a read-through; target approximately 3 minutes 15 seconds.
- **Speaking share:** Leah 70% of spoken words, Chris 30%, calculated per episode (not merely the number of turns).
- **LEAH:** Indigenous presenter, speaking from her own Walpole Island First Nation perspective; primary narrator of the Call's significance, community implications, and accountability questions. Do not invent personal experiences, teachings, quotations, or claim she speaks for all Indigenous people.
- **CHRIS:** Non-Indigenous ally, adding substantive context, questions, responsibilities and concrete ally actions without displacing Indigenous leadership.
- **Accuracy:** Check each Call against the original TRC text (https://nctr.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Calls_to_Action_English2.pdf). Preserve all material requirements and name the correct responsible parties. Separate verified outcomes from announcements and proposals. Source and date any progress claims. Do not imply national data describe Bkejwanong specifically.
- **Structure:** Hook; what the Call actually requests; why it matters; documented status and uncertainty; hard accountability question; audience action; closing: “Hard Truths. Education. Accountability.”
- **QPrompt format:** Speaker labels **LEAH:** and **CHRIS:** uppercase and bold on their own line, dialogue immediately on the next line, blank line between thoughts and speaker turns; minimal headings, stage directions on separate lines.
- **Editorial workflow:** Rewrite call by call; count spoken words by host and estimate/read-test duration; verify facts and sources; mark unverified claims for review. Preserve existing published scripts until replacements pass review. Avoid generic interchangeable scripts.
- **Release:** Do not claim a script is final or published until its episode has been checked. Changes to the site require separate safe edits and deployment verification.
