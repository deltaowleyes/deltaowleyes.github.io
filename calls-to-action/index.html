The 94 Calls to Action

Explore the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada's 94 Calls to Action. Each numbered entry identifies its subject and connects readers to the official document. Detailed, evidence-based accountability profiles will be added as research is verified.

Editorial transparency: A call's presence here does not indicate completion or lack of progress. Status assessments require dated evidence and clear criteria. This independent educational resource is not an official TRC or First Nation publication.

How we'll investigate each call

Each forthcoming profile will include the original wording, a plain-language explanation, historical context, accountable institutions, dated progress evidence, unresolved issues, Indigenous perspectives where appropriately sourced, and links to related Delta Owl Eyes reporting. Sources and update dates will be visible.
