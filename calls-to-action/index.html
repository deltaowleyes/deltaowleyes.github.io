How accountability is assessed

Independent assessment as of October 1, 2026: 14 complete, 42 in progress, 23 stalled and 15 not started (Indigenous Watchdog). These are third-party classifications, not Delta Owl Eyes findings. Each call needs evidence of action, evidence of outcomes, Indigenous-led perspectives and dated sources. Government announcements are not proof of completion. We will publish a status only after reviewing the full recommendation and its measurable requirements.
