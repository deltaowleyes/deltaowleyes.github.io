Indigenous Church Directory

Discover churches across Canada that are Indigenous-led or operated, located on a First Nation reserve, or both. Listings are free. Indigenous leadership and reserve location are separate classifications and are not assumed from one another.

Only communities with preliminary church records appear in the community filter. Listings with neither Indigenous leadership nor reserve location confirmed are research leads, not yet eligibility-verified directory entries. An unlisted community does not mean there are no churches there.

Verification policy: These are preliminary listings based on published community or denominational sources. Contact details, schedules, and Indigenous leadership are not independently confirmed. Please contact the church before visiting. To request a correction or add a church, email deltaowleyes@gmail.com.
