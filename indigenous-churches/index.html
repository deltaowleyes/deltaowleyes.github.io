Indigenous Church Directory

Discover churches across Canada that are Indigenous-led or operated, located on a First Nation reserve, or both. Listings are free. Indigenous leadership and reserve location are separate classifications and are not assumed from one another.

Verification policy: These are preliminary listings based on published community or denominational sources. Contact details, schedules, and Indigenous leadership are not independently confirmed.
