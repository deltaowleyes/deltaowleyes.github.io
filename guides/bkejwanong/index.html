Bkejwanong (Walpole Island) Community Resources

This community guide offers links to publicly available resources for Bkejwanong (Walpole Island First Nation), including sourced church listings, a searchable business directory and official community event information. Delta Owl Eyes is an independent initiative and does not represent the First Nation government.

Compiled from the public Delta Owl Eyes research directories on October 31, 2026. Sources and verification status may change. Please verify details with the relevant organization.

Explore community businesses

The Indigenous Business Directory can be searched using Walpole Island or Bkejwanong in the search field. Where data is available, use the city, community and category filters. Listings are source-collected research records until businesses confirm their details.

Business owners can request a listing correction or claim; basic entries are free.

Community events and announcements

For public activities, workshops, treaty education, meetings and cultural events, start with the official Walpole Island First Nation events page. The Delta Owl Eyes Community Events Calendar also lists sourced public events and links back to organizers.

Event schedules, participation rules and locations may change. Some gatherings are not public; check with the organizer before attending, and respect privacy and cultural access requirements.
