Ontario Indigenous Community Resource Guide

This province guide connects three Delta Owl Eyes resources in one place: Indigenous business listings, church research records, and community events. It is a navigational guide—not an official First Nation directory or a certification of any listing.

Compiled from the public Delta Owl Eyes research directories on October 31, 2026. Sources and verification status may change. Please verify details with the relevant organization.

Find Indigenous businesses in Ontario

Search the Indigenous Business Directory and choose Ontario under Province. Narrow the results by city, community and business category. Each public record includes its verification status and available source or contact information. Search results change as new records are added; this page deliberately does not copy the entire directory.

Listings marked Unverified have not been directly confirmed with Delta Owl Eyes. A publicly cited source does not by itself establish current operation, Indigenous ownership or on-reserve location.

Community events and organizer sources

For dates, public attendance requirements and changes, consult the live Indigenous Community Events Calendar and the event organizer directly. Current Ontario research records include treaty education, arts workshops, sports and wellness, community commemoration and cultural gatherings.

  • Walpole Island First Nation community events — official community calendar.
  • Ontario Treaties Recognition Week — provincial educational information and scheduled programming.
  • National Arts Centre Indigenous Theatre — organizer programme listings.
  • Indigenous Sport & Wellness Ontario — event directory and activity information.

These organizer URLs are present in Delta Owl Eyes' sourced event records. Verify individual event dates and access conditions before travelling.
