Northern Ontario First Nation Church Research Guide

Northern Ontario church information is spread across diocesan, denominational and community websites. This research guide offers a readable selection of source-listed entries and links to the originating publishers. It does not certify current services, staffing, Indigenous leadership or reserve location.

Compiled from the public Delta Owl Eyes research directories on October 31, 2026. Sources and verification status may change. Please verify details with the relevant organization.

How the church records are classified

Each record names a church, a community and its reported tradition. The public Indigenous Church Directory separately tracks whether leadership is Indigenous and whether the church is on a First Nation reserve. Those classifications are not inferred from a denominational source alone. Some records remain unverified research leads, so confirm details with the church or source before visiting.
