Bkejwanong businesses and services: source-backed listings

This selective guide brings together six organizations listed in the public Walpole Island First Nation community directory. Grouped by service type, it helps visitors find food, retail, tourism, health, transportation and economic-development contacts.

Source review: October 31, 2026. Delta Owl Eyes is independent and does not speak for Walpole Island First Nation. Names are listed for research; operation, eligibility, ownership and addresses require confirmation.

What we verify—and what we do not

Documented: the name and listing are present in the public First Nation directory. Not independently confirmed: whether each operation is currently open, who owns it, and whether the operating location is on-reserve. These are separate attributes in the Delta Owl Eyes research workflow. A listing is not an endorsement or a claim of Indigenous ownership.

Are you listed here? Request a correction or claim. We do not publish private residential addresses or individual contact details on this page.
