Indigenous Business Showcase

Source-backed community event information. This page explains what the event is, where and when it is scheduled, who the invitation is intended for, and how to check the organizer's original notice.

Researched October 31, 2026. Dates and eligibility may change; always consult the source before travel or registration.

Event details

Date: Thursday–Friday, November 19–20, 2026

Time: November 19 at 9:30 a.m. to November 20 at 5:30 p.m. (Eastern Time), as listed by the community source; check the detailed schedule with the organizers.

Location: Four Points by Sheraton, London, Ontario, 1150 Wellington Road

Official community listing: Indigenous Business Showcase on Walpole Island First Nation's website.

Purpose and participation

The Walpole Island First Nation notice shares the event for local entrepreneurs interested in learning, connecting and networking. The community post is a source for the listing, not confirmation that Walpole Island First Nation is the event organizer.

Entrepreneurs should consult the official event notice for participation rules and registration requirements. No free admission or eligibility is assumed here.

Before visiting

Confirm the event is still scheduled and ask the organizer about registration, accessibility, participation criteria and any changes to the venue. Delta Owl Eyes does not independently operate or administer this event.

Check the current official event notice.
