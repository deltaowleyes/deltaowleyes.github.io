Bkejwanong KWEWAG Engagement

Source-backed community event information. This page explains what the event is, where and when it is scheduled, who the invitation is intended for, and how to check the organizer's original notice.

Researched October 31, 2026. Dates and eligibility may change; always consult the source before travel or registration.

Event details

Date: Thursday, November 12, 2026

Time: 5:30–8:30 p.m. (Eastern Time)

Location: Walpole Arena Back Hall, Walpole Island, Ontario

Community organizer: Walpole Island First Nation

Official community listing: Bkejwanong KWEWAG Engagement on Walpole Island First Nation's website.

Purpose and participation

The organizer welcomes Bkejwanong women of all ages to contribute to the community’s Bkejwanong Biish Gchi-Wiindamaagewin (Water Declaration). The posted notice states that dinner is included.

This is a community engagement session about the Water Declaration, not a general public festival. Respect the invitation criteria and ask the organizer about access and participation before attending.

Before visiting

Confirm the event is still scheduled and ask the organizer about registration, accessibility, participation criteria and any changes to the venue. Delta Owl Eyes does not independently operate or administer this event.
