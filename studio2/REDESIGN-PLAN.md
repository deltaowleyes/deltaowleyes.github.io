## Original inspirational references (style/UX research; no copying)
1. **IndigiNews** — https://indiginews.com/ — Indigenous-centred editorial storytelling, community voices and respectful treatment of sensitive topics. Design lesson: foreground real stories and cultural context.
2. **The Narwhal** — https://thenarwhal.ca/ — clear editorial hierarchy, photography and long-form investigative storytelling. Its Oct. 29, 2026 homepage update describes topic-based sections and digestible short-news formats: https://thenarwhal.ca/audience-product-updates-2026/
3. **APTN News** — https://www.aptnnews.ca/ — discoverable video programs, interviews and investigations as a first-class newsroom experience.
4. **ProPublica** — https://www.propublica.org/ — investigation packages linking stories to methodologies, explainers and related material, as described in its May 5, 2026 redesign note https://www.propublica.org/article/why-propublica-redesign.

These are attributed **references only**. Do not copy HTML/CSS, screenshots, photography, logos, fonts, text or source assets from their sites.

## Six proposed independently architected Delta Owl Eyes design families
1. **River Voice (editorial flagship):** photographic front page, lead reporting, featured topic strips, quiet serif type, newspaper rhythm. Pilot at `/studio2/river-voice/`.
2. **Modern Longhouse (community hub):** communal spatial navigation, gathering/program calendar as center, radial pathways, warm timber/gold design (not a copied ceremonial representation).
3. **Accountability Ledger (investigative):** monochrome-plus-red evidence dossiers, document timelines, methodology notes, investigative taxonomy, prominent source citations.
4. **Northern Horizons (cultural):** immersive landscape storytelling, land/people/culture discovery, photographic essays and captions; prevent fake cultural representations.
5. **Community Network (directory):** searchable business/church/event pathways, district/location navigation, service cards, verified/unverified states.
6. **Media Hub (video/podcast):** large program video masthead, series rails, episode libraries, interview pages and captions/transcripts where available.

For **each family**, six genuine adaptations must change page geometry, navigation system, content sequence, typography, and interaction; not just colors. Exact variant briefs are to be approved following the flagship's visual acceptance.
