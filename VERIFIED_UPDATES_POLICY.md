# Delta Owl Eyes — Verified publication policy

## Scope
This policy governs proposed factual updates to the 94 Calls to Action articles and recording scripts.

## Recording and news lifecycle
- Scripts awaiting recording may appear in the editorial script queue.
- Once Chris and Leah mark a video as published and provide its video URL, remove that script from the public-facing script queue and replace its presentation with the published video link or embed. Do not delete the underlying script: retain a dated editorial archive and Git history.
- A published video and its historical script are immutable records of what was reported at that time; do not silently rewrite them in response to later developments.
- New verified developments after publication must generate a **new, standalone news script**, dated and linked to the affected Call to Action and earlier coverage. Never recycle or overwrite a previously recorded script.
- Keep evergreen Call to Action information current separately, with a dated update log. Distinguish later corrections to previous reporting from genuinely new developments.
- Only transition a script to published status after explicit publication confirmation or verifiable published video URL. A script draft alone is not proof of publication.


## Automatic publication eligibility
A revision may be published without manual editorial approval only when all conditions hold:
- The change is a narrow factual correction or dated status update, not a new allegation, opinion, quotation, or substantive reinterpretation.
- The exact affected Call to Action is identified.
- The claim is supported by a directly linked primary source (such as the responsible government, legislation, official program record, or the original issuing organization) with an access date and, where available, publication date.
- The source actually supports the specific wording. No claim of implementation or completion is inferred solely from announcements, funding commitments, or plans.
- The original text, revised text, source URL, verification timestamp, and reason for change are recorded.
- Validation succeeds and no conflicting authoritative evidence is detected.

## Hold for review
Do not auto-publish disputed completion statuses, conflicting sources, new allegations about identifiable people or institutions, legal interpretations, Indigenous cultural claims requiring community input, or changes to the editorial position of Delta Owl Eyes.

## Integrity
- Preserve all 94 Call numbers, navigation, shared header, banner, branding, and existing article URLs.
- Maintain matching facts across the article and its 3–7-minute recording script.
- Label dates clearly and never silently overwrite historical status.
- Do not represent automated drafts as independently verified.
- Keep a reversible Git commit history and change log.

## Operational note
This document is a policy, not a functioning research or publishing bot. Scheduled monitoring in ChatGPT does not itself trigger a GitHub commit. Automatic publication requires a separately deployed integration that fetches primary sources, verifies structured changes, validates content, and commits to the site. Until that integration exists, no automatic publishing is claimed.
